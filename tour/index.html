Crypto Anomaly Pipeline

Every five minutes a Lambda downloads one-minute price bars for 17 crypto pairs from Binance. Spark on Glue cleans them, then flags minutes where a pair moved or traded far outside its own recent normal. The output is a filter for a person to review, not a trading signal.

How extreme: |z| distribution

Under a normal distribution, |z| above 4 would be rare (about 1 in 16,000 minutes). Here it is common. Market returns have fat tails.

Largest events

Return events cluster on US-market minutes

An average minute of the day holds about 15 return events over the full period. A few minutes hold far more. US Eastern times are daylight time, in force until 1 November. These line up with known market times; the cause has not been tested (M-059).
